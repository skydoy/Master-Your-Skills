Write the message so the reader can act without replying with questions.

## Mode

- **Direct**: the user described the problem and who to send it to. Draft at once.
- **Guided**: details missing. Ask one numbered round, max 6 questions, each with a suggested answer: who is the reader [IT support], what exactly happened [?], who or what is affected [me only], what you tried [restart], what you need [fix or workaround], deadline [end of week]. Show a 4-6 line brief (goal, reader, tone, length) first. Max 2 rounds.

Write the message in the reader's language; reply to the user in theirs.

## Do this

1. Pick the register: boss = short, decision-ready, impact first; IT = exact symptoms, steps, error text; supplier = order or contract reference, facts, polite firmness.
2. Build the message in this order:
   - **Subject**: problem plus object plus urgency, for example "Printer 2nd floor not printing, 5 staff blocked".
   - **What happened**: when it started, what you saw, exact error text.
   - **Impact**: who or what is stopped, cost or deadline at risk.
   - **What I tried**: bullets, so nobody repeats them.
   - **What I need**: one clear ask (fix, approval, refund, replacement) and by when, with the reason for that date.
   - **Attachments**: list suggested ones, such as a screenshot of the full error, order number, photo, invoice, ticket ID, version or device name.
3. Keep it under 150 words for a boss, under 250 otherwise. One request per message.
4. Use only facts the user gave. Missing items become [PLACEHOLDERS] and are listed at the end.
5. Tone: calm and specific. No blame, no threats, no stock openers or closers. If the user is angry, state the facts and keep the firmness in the deadline.

## Done when

- The ask and the date are in the first half of the message.
- A stranger could start work from it.
- Attachments are named.

## Traps

- Burying the ask under the story.
- "It does not work": say what it does instead.
- Asking for three things in one message.

End with "Check before sending": fill placeholders, attach the listed files, confirm the recipient and the deadline are realistic.
