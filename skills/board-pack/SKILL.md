---
name: board-pack
description: Assembles a board or committee meeting pack from loose inputs: agenda, a paper per item with decision requested, KPIs vs target, risks, appendices; flags what is missing. Use for "board pack", "committee papers", "pauta da reunião", "pacote para o conselho".
---

Build one coherent pack a director can read in the order of the meeting, and say what is still missing.

## Mode
- **Direct**: agenda or materials given. Assemble now.
- **Guided**: one numbered round, max 6 questions, each with a bracketed suggestion: body and date [the next regular meeting], agenda items [from the material], who presents each [the item owner], KPI list and targets [from the last pack], confidential items [none], language [the user's]. Show a 4-6 line brief first. Max 2 rounds.

## Do this
1. Inventory every input. Map each to an agenda item or to the appendix. Anything that fits nowhere is listed under "Unplaced material".
2. Build the pack in this order:
   - **Cover**: body, date, time, place, attendees, apologies [VALUE].
   - **Agenda**: number, title, presenter, minutes, and a tag: Decide, Discuss or Note. Total time must fit the meeting.
   - **Papers**: one page per item: purpose in a sentence, background (3 lines), options or analysis, **Decision requested** in exact resolution wording ("The board is asked to approve..."), financial and legal impact, owner. Note items have no decision line.
   - **KPI table**: KPI, actual, target, variance, trend, one-line comment. Mark red/amber/green only by a stated rule.
   - **Risks**: top risks with owner, rating, mitigation, change since last meeting.
   - **Minutes and actions** from the last meeting, with status.
   - **Appendices list**: title, source, page count.
3. Run the gap check and place it first, before the cover: missing papers, items with no decision wording, KPIs without a target or source, risks without an owner, empty minutes, over-length agenda, unsigned-off financials.
4. Keep each paper short; long detail goes to an appendix, not the paper.

## Rules
- Never invent figures, names, dates or resolutions. Gaps become [VALUE] and appear in the gap check.
- Neutral, formal register. Papers state facts, then ask; they do not lobby.
- Pack language follows the board's language.

## Stop and ask when
- The decision wording would commit the organisation and no owner has approved it.

## Done when
- Every Decide item has a decision wording and an owner.
- The gap check lists everything still open.
- Agenda timings add up to the meeting length.

## End with
**Check before sending**: confidentiality markings; figures tie to the source reports; legal or finance sign-off where needed; circulation deadline met.
