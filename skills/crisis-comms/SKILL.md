description: Fast, facts-only drafts for an incident: internal note, customer or public statement, press holding line and FAQ, with what we know, don't know and next update time. Use for "incident statement", "outage message", "press line", "comunicado de crise", "nota de imprensa".
---

Give the team usable text in minutes, built only on confirmed facts. Speed first, then polish.

## Mode
- **Direct**: facts given. Draft all four at once.
- **Guided**: one numbered round, max 6 questions, each with a bracketed suggestion, only for what is missing: what happened and when [as stated], who is affected [unknown], current status [investigating], next update time [in 60 minutes], spokesperson [the user], language per audience [the audience's]. Skip the brief; show the facts table instead. Max 1 round, then draft with placeholders.

## Do this
1. Build a **fact table** first: Confirmed / Not yet known / Do not say. Every sentence you write must trace to Confirmed.
2. Draft in this order, each short:
   - **Internal note**: what happened, impact, what staff should do, what they must not say or post, where questions go, next update time.
   - **Customer or public statement**: what happened in plain words, who is affected, what we are doing, what you should do, next update time. Say sorry once, specifically, if people were harmed or inconvenienced.
   - **Press holding line**: 2-3 sentences. Acknowledge, state the confirmed fact, say we are investigating, give a contact. No speculation.
   - **FAQ**: 5-8 likely questions with answers; an unknown gets "We do not know yet; we will update at [TIME]".
3. Each text has a **What we know / What we don't know yet / Next update at [TIME]** block. Commit to a time you can keep.
4. Match the language of each audience. Plain words, no jargon, no blame, no minimising ("minor", "isolated") unless confirmed.
5. Put a banner on top: **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED before release**, with the reasons that apply: personal data, safety, liability, regulators, litigation risk, listed-company disclosure.

## Rules
- Never invent facts, causes, numbers, times, names or quotes. Gaps become [FACT] or [TIME] and are listed at the end.
- No promises you cannot keep (no "this will never happen again"), and no cause until confirmed.
- Statements that name people or customers, or admit fault, go to legal first.

## Stop and ask when
- Nobody can confirm what happened or who is affected: draft only the holding line and internal note.

## Done when
- All four items drafted, each traceable to confirmed facts.
- A next update time is in every text.
- Legal review is flagged and placeholders are listed.

## End with
**Check before sending**: legal sign-off; facts reconfirmed with the incident lead; one spokesperson; update time owner named.
