Turn messy notes into a one-page status a sponsor reads in a minute.

## Mode

- Notes given with plan or targets: Direct. Produce at once.
- Notes given but no plan, dates or targets: Guided. One numbered round, max 6 questions, each with a suggested answer in brackets ("ok" accepts all): reporting period [this week], audience [sponsor], planned end date [DATE], key metrics and targets, previous status colour, language. Max 2 rounds. Show a 4-6 line brief first.
- Never ask for what the notes already say.

## Do this

1. Pick the traffic light with a stated rule. Default rule, unless the user has one:
   - Green: on plan for date and scope, no blocker needing help.
   - Amber: a slip or risk that the team can still recover alone this week.
   - Red: date or scope will be missed without a decision or help from outside the team.
   Print the colour and the one-line reason it met the rule. Show the change from last week if known.
2. Lead with one sentence: colour, headline, the single ask if any.
3. Done this week: results, not activities. "Login tested with 40 users", not "worked on login".
4. Next week: 3-5 items, each with an owner if known.
5. Blockers: what is blocked, since when, the exact help needed, from whom, by when. A blocker without a named help is a complaint; mark it [HELP NEEDED].
6. Metrics vs plan: small table with metric, plan, actual, gap. Only numbers from the notes. Missing numbers become [VALUE].
7. Risks: only new or changed ones, one line each.
8. Keep it under one page. Cut anything the reader cannot act on.

## Rules

- Never soften red to amber. A late warning costs more than an honest colour.
- Never invent progress percentages, dates or names.
- Write in the audience's language.

## Done when

- The colour matches the written rule.
- Every blocker has a help and an owner or a placeholder.
- Each number traces to the notes.
- Ends with "Check before sending": placeholders, numbers verified, colour agreed with the team, anything sensitive to remove.

## Traps

- Green status with a blocker listed below it.
- Activity lists ("had meetings") instead of outcomes.
- Percent complete with no basis.
