name: okr
description: Converts strategy text into 3-5 objectives, each with 2-4 measurable key results, owners, baselines and targets; rejects activities disguised as results. Use for "write OKRs", "turn strategy into goals", "metas trimestrais", "objetivos e resultados-chave".
---

Turn direction into goals you can score at the end of the period, not a task list.

## Mode
- **Direct**: strategy and period given. Draft now.
- **Guided**: one numbered round, max 6 questions, each with a bracketed suggestion: period [next quarter], level [company], owners available [infer from the text], current numbers [list what you can find], ambition [stretch, 70% counts as success], language [the user's]. Show a 4-6 line brief before drafting. Max 2 rounds.

## Do this
1. Extract the few outcomes the strategy truly wants. Drop what is only "keep running the business".
2. Write 3-5 objectives: qualitative, inspiring, one sentence, no numbers, achievable inside the period.
3. For each, write 2-4 key results. Each KR has: metric, **baseline**, **target**, deadline, owner, data source. Format: "Raise [metric] from [baseline] to [target] by [date]".
4. Test every KR with three questions. Reject and rewrite it if any answer is no:
   - Is it a number that moves, not an action? "Launch the portal", "run training" and "hold 10 meetings" are activities. Ask what changes because of it, and measure that.
   - Could it be scored 0-100% at the end by someone not involved?
   - Does the owner control or strongly influence it?
5. Show a rejection log: the original activity, the verdict, the rewritten KR. If the outcome is unmeasurable today, make the first KR "establish the baseline for X by [date]" and flag it.
6. Check balance: no objective has only one lever; KRs do not repeat each other; one owner has at most 2 objectives; do not all measure output, include quality or cost.
7. Output a table: Objective, KR, owner, baseline, target, source, confidence (1-10).

## Rules
- Never invent baselines or targets. Missing numbers become [BASELINE] or [TARGET] and are listed at the end with who can supply them.
- Distinguish committed goals (expect 100%) from aspirational ones (expect about 70%); label each.
- No filler words such as "leverage", "synergy", "robust". Write in the user's language.

## Stop and ask when
- The strategy has no outcome that can be turned into a number.

## Done when
- 3-5 objectives, 2-4 KRs each, every KR with owner, baseline, target, date.
- No activity remains disguised as a result.
- Missing numbers are listed.

## End with
**Check before using**: baselines checked against real data; owners agreed; targets realistic against capacity; review cadence set.
